# Synthetic phase calibration frozen specification

Purpose: characterize the accepted rank fixed QR mean three pair correlation statistic on new synthetic data under a valid random phase null and two predeclared stress mechanisms. No real data or accepted results are read. The original .001 empirical tails are not a validation target.

## Fixed generator and statistic
128 ordered windows on one synthetic ring, denominator 32 counts each. Three independent fields G P S. For each field draw 128 iid standard normal innovations, apply the circular five tap symmetric kernel [1,2,3,2,1]/sqrt(19), and multiply by sigma_i. Counts=round(32 logistic(z_i)), integer0..32. A fresh uniform circular phase is applied only in S1 and S3. Seeds are NumPy SeedSequence([20261028,scenario_number,replicate_number]), PCG64, replicates0..99. Generator and inner offset draws share only that replicate local RNG. No seed search or replacement. P remains fixed inside all inner shifts; G and S are independently rotated.

X has intercept and four fixed columns sin(2pi i/128),cos(2pi i/128),sin(4pi i/128),cos(4pi i/128). One unchanged original fixed_qr per stage, exact original average tie ranks/response_residuals and residual Pearson functions. T=(r_GP+r_GS+r_PS)/3. Observed identity is retained; each99 inner draws samples shift pairs with replacement independently. Tail=(1+count(T_inner>=T_observed-1e-12))/100. No redraw of undefined vectors: first undefined/error HOLD. Retain all100 statistics per replicate.

## Three fixed scenarios
S1 valid phase null: sigma=1 everywhere, fresh independent uniform initial rotations G/P/S; inner shifts uniform0..127. Conditional on each generated orbit and P, G/S phases are iid uniform over cyclic product group; identity plus iid uniform group elements gives valid conservative randomized upper tail including ties. Every species distribution is invariant under count complement32-c; rank complement maps residual to negative because X contains intercept. In ideal generator and exact arithmetic with a defined statistic, cross species independence and complement sign symmetry give E[T]=0. Conditional orbit mean zero also holds for this harmonic design because its projection commutes with cyclic rotations; this centering is not the justification of randomization validity. This is the sole exact transformation benchmark.
S2 nonstationary zero shared signal stress: sigma=3 for indices48..79 and .2 otherwise; no initial rotations, same fixed variance envelope for all3 independent innovation fields. Inner shifts uniform0..127. Complement symmetry and independence still give E[T]=0; nonstationary variance breaks phase invariance. Rejection frequency describes this particular misspecified null, not calibrated false positive control for real data.
S3 restricted allowed set stress: same generated process and initial phases as S1, but inner shifts uniform16..111 inclusive. This offset set excludes near identity and is not closed under addition mod128. This is a bin level proxy for phase exclusions, NOT the real genomic protection/boundary algorithm. Its behavior cannot establish validity of real restrictions.
